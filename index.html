About Me And My Work

Hello, I go by Vilppi, Vil or Vi! I am an 18 year old digital artist coming from Finland and I love to meet new people! I currently study in a game development and animation focused school, but art has been with me all the way from elementary school. I love art and always have, and drawing has become my strongest passion! As an artist I am always open to new esxperiences and learning something new.

My artstyle has shaped itself for years and is still improving thanks to my passion! Being in an art focused school has only made my passion grow stronger.

I hope I can share my passion with you!
If you're interested in commissioning me you can contact me via my discord, email or Ko-Fi!
